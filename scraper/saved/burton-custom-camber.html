    Have a Question?

    A legendary poppy, versatile, and fun board with twin flex and camber that make short work of all terrain and conditions.

    From its humble beginnings, innovation has defined the Burton Custom Snowboard series and set it apart as the most popular, versatile, and mimicked board in snowboarding. Since 1996, this icon has reigned supreme while constantly evolving with a proven formula that combines time-honored design with next-gen ingredients to create a lightweight, poppy, and highly versatile board. Iridescent ink adds a shifting sheen to the graphic to match the board's legendary performance. The Custom Camber is the top choice for many Burton pro riders.

    About The Graphic

    Faded
    Misty and minimal at first glance, the Custom's spray paint fade is done by hand and packs a bold, powerful visual to match the board's fast, fun, and versatile ride. A bright gradient base lights up the snow for extra pop.

    Jungle
    Guided by the Custom's fast, fun, and versatile ride, a jungle scene plays up a ride-to-paradise theme, sharing the board's eclectic personality through colorful imagery.

    • Powerful and Precise

      Our Camber Bend encourages powerful turns and poppy precision.

    • Classic Directional Shape

      A twin flex balances the directional shape for an all-terrain ride that you can ride regular or switch.

    • Compatibility

      The Channel™ Board Mount is compatible with all major bindings and lets you dial in the stance exactly the way you like it.

    Benefits

    Terrain

    Park/Freestyle
    Designed for jumps, rails, side hits, and creative riding on the hill and in the streets. Freestyle-friendly flexes, twin shapes, and a forgiving feel make it easier to butter, press, spin, and progress, while being built to take a beating, lap after lap.

    All Mountain
    Built to handle everything and then some. From groomers and park laps to powder, trees, chutes, and everything in between, these versatile boards balance stability, playfulness, and response so you can ride it all, in any condition.

    Powder
    Made for speed and flow in deep snow. Directional shapes and powder-specific designs help the board float so your legs stay fresh for a smoother, more effortless ride through soft snow, natural terrain, and every pillow-popping, surfy line you can find."

    Personality (Happy Medium)

    Soft & Playful Happy Medium Stiff & Aggressive
    Benefits

    Personality

    Soft & Playful
    Whether you’re learning the basics, lapping the park, or prefer a smoother ride, a softer flex is easier to control because it bends with you. The result is a more forgiving ride that absorbs every feature and landing while softening the ones that don’t quite go to plan.

    Happy Medium
    Like to ride a bit of everything? A medium flex combines soft and playful control with increased response, giving you pop to hit features and edge hold to carve whatever the mountain throws your way.

    Stiff & Aggressive
    Charging steep lines, laying out carves, riding fast enough to warp space and time? A stiffer flex locks in at speed for response and precision so you can push harder, go faster, and fully commit on every turn."

    Tech Details

    • Bend

      Camber

      A Camber Bend advocates powerful turns and poppy precision, offering snappy suspension with weight distributed evenly across the entire board for smooth, continuous edge control from tip to tail

      Bends

      Camber

      A Camber Bend advocates powerful turns and poppy precision, offering snappy suspension with weight distributed evenly across the entire board for smooth, continuous edge control from tip to tail

      Directional Camber

      Directional Camber Bend has camber under both feet for stability through deep-diving turns and variable conditions and a rockered nose that adds lift for increased float

      Directional Flat Top

      Directional Flat Top Bend features a flat profile overall with a rocker nose for the stability and effortless float perfect for deep days on surfy terrain

      Flat Top

      Flat Top Bend provides stability, balance, and continuous edge control helping the tip and tail kick up for a catch-free and loose riding experience

      Flying V™

      Flying V Bend features rocker zones between and outside your feet for enhanced playfulness, and float and camber zones underneath your feet that focus edge control for crisp snap, added pop, and powerful turns

      PurePop Camber

      PurePop Camber Bend features subtle flat zones to amplify pop and add playfulness

      V-Rocker™

      V-Rocker Bend rockers up in the middle with flat zones underneath the bindings and a continuous rocker outside the binding area to create an easy-riding, surfy feel.

    • Shape

      All Mountain Directional Shape

      Directional Shape is designed to be ridden with a slightly longer nose than tail to concentrate pop in the tail while providing plenty of float, flow, and control to rip any terrain or condition

      Shapes

      All Mountain Directional Shape

      Directional Shape is designed to be ridden with a slightly longer nose than tail to concentrate pop in the tail while providing plenty of float, flow, and control to rip any terrain or condition

      Freeride Directional Shape

      Freeride Directional Shape features a setback camber and a sidecut that is centered on your stance to create a twin freestyle stance when riding flat base, and on edge it turns tight and quick with the float and prowess of a directional deck

      Twin Shape

      Twin Shape is perfectly symmetrical for a balanced ride so you can spin and stomp with outstanding stability whether you're riding regular or switch

    • Flex

      Twin Flex

      Twin Flex is perfectly symmetrical from tip to tail for a balanced ride that's equally versatile regular or switch

      Flex

      Directional Flex

      Directional Flex features increased pop in the tail and a more resilient nose that allows riders to easily maintain control through all terrain and conditions

      Twin Flex

      Twin Flex is perfectly symmetrical from tip to tail for a balanced ride that's equally versatile regular or switch

    • Core

      Super Fly II™ 700G Core

      Super Fly II 700G Core uses stronger and lighter wood to provide pop and strength while reducing overall weight

      Cores

      Dragonfly II™ 500G Core

      Dragonfly II 500G core is Burton's lightest Core ever, integrating carbon laminations inside the core to provide absolute pop and performance with far less material

      Dragonfly™ 600G Core

      Dragonfly 600G Core with Multizone EGD uses end-grain woods in impact zones for lighter weight that doesn't sacrifice strength

      Super Fly II™ 700G Core

      Super Fly II 700G Core uses stronger and lighter wood to provide pop and strength while reducing overall weight

      Super Fly® 800G Core

      Super Fly 800G Core is a dual-species wood core that is loaded with pop and alternates between hard and soft woods to reduce overall weight without limiting performance

      Fly® Core

      Fly 900G Core is a classic tip-to-tail wood core that lightens the load without sacrificing flex, pop, or strength

    • Core

      Dualzone™ EGD™

      Dualzone EGD engineered wood grain is positioned along the toe and heel edges on two continuous zones perpendicular to the core for more edge-hold, response, and strength
