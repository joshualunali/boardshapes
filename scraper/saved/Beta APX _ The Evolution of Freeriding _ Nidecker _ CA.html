Key Features

The Evolution of Freeriding

The Beta APX adds top drawer materials to an organic silhouette to create the ultimate backcountry dart. Plenty of volume in the nose provides surf-like float, while extra carbon, thinner sidewalls and a faster base bring even more speed and precision. A favourite of Japanese soul shredder Shin Biyajima, this is the perfect board for riders who enjoy following their instincts in natural terrain – exploring new lines and subtle new ways to turn.
  • The Beta APX's pinched-in swallow tail maximises volume and running length where you need it, while effortlessly sinking in pow – eliminating back-leg burn.
  • The upgraded sidewall construction delivers power into the edge faster and maximises grip for super precise turns.
  • Full-length carbon inlays amplify the board's response and keep the ride smooth even when you're pushing mach 10.

Shape and Profile

Surfy CamRock - Features extended rocker at the nose and regular camber through the rest of the board. Together with a shorter tail, it allows you to glide over bumps or pow and initiate turns off the back foot for a unique, surf-like ride.

Tech Details

  • Construction

    Power Rails

    An exclusive Nidecker construction inspired by surfboards. The topsheet is wrapped over a rounded wood core to meet a thin sidewall. It's a beautiful finish that's even more stunning to ride, delivering instant drive through the edge.

  • Topsheet

    Absorbnid

    An innovative top layer designed to absorb unwanted vibrations. Enjoy a smooth ride with less fatigue.

  • Inserts

    4x2/6

    6 rows of inserts at 2 cm intervals gives you maximum choice of stance width and works with any binding on the market.

  • Fiberglass

    Triax Plus

    Fiberglass is woven in three directions at 45° for max stiffness. The result of extensive testing, Triax Plus uses different glass densities above and below the core to deliver more consistent torsional flex, better pop, and lightning response.

  • Core

    Light Core

    An ultra lightweight core based on a lively combination of poplar and paulownia. Hardwood beech stringers down the centre enhance the overall strength and boost pop.

  • Base

    N-9000 Sintered

    A lightning quick sintered base made from super dense material with a high carbon content to minimise friction. Wax it on the regs for race-level performance.

  • Added Carbon

    Pop Carbon Plus

    Pop Carbon is deployed on both sides of the core and from tip to tail, beefing up the flex. Ideal for aggressive riders who want snappy edge response, tons of pop and a smooth ride at high speeds.

  • 3D Shaping

    Drop Out

    The Drop Out is derived from the shape of a bird of prey's tail. It generates more control when up on edge, but also allows for extra agility and deep, tight turns.

  • 3D Shaping

    Evolution nose

    Our 3D curved nose helps the board roll smoothly from edge to edge, creating a natural, surf-like flow through each turn.

Extras

  • Diecut Base

    Pure-color base materials pieced together like a jigsaw for bold, crisp graphics.

  • Gloss Finish

    Adds a premium deep shine to select board artwork.

  • Overprint

    Layered topsheet artwork that adds depth and texture to the graphic.

  • Translucent Color

    A three-dimensional graphic using less ink to reveal more of the construction.
